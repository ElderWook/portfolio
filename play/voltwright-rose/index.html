The three main connections — grounded in real standards

The web teaches three real connection domains — but they are not three co-equal spokes (that was the 3-fold we rejected). They are the anatomy of a differential link: the two wings are the two conductors, the ground spine is the reference, and Comms is the signal the pair carries. Muscle · mind · nerves.

left wing · muscle

AC Power Distribution

Connections: service entrance → panelboard / busbar → feeder → breaker → Δ-Y transformer → branch → load; grounding electrode + bonding jumper.
Standard: phase-ID by voltage class — mandated neutral white/grey · EGC green · high-leg orange; phases by convention (208 V blk·red·blu / 480 V brn·org·yel); MV by stripe-count. phase-identification.md · conductor-sizing.md
Teaches: get energy source→load safely — ampacity, grounding & bonding, Δ-Y.

right wing · mind

PCB Logic

Connections: power rail (+3V3 / +5V) · GND pour / plane · signal trace · bus · clock · decoupling · via · header.
Standard: Okabe-Ito net-role colours (colourblind-safe) · controlled impedance · the stackup. ART-BIBLE.md · skin.py
Teaches: the brain that decides — routing, planes, decoupling, net roles.

the axis · nerves

Comms — the signal on the pair

Connections: differential buses — USB 90 Ω (board controlled-Z) · RS-485/Modbus & CAN 120 Ω (terminate both ends + bias) · Ethernet 100 Ω; PLC I/O, −48 VDC telecom; the card-edge cluster bus (CBUS_H/L · SYNC_CLK) = the co-op link.
Standard: Zdiff + terminate + length-match + continuous reference plane + symmetric vias. differential-pairs.md
Teaches: common-mode rejection — the signature "you actually learned this," recurring USB → RS-485/CAN → Ethernet.

The unification. The two wings are the two conductors of a differential pair; the central spine is the ground reference plane; Comms is the differential signal carried on the pair, closing at the PLC capstone — a board (logic) that talks to gear (power) over a bus. Every diff-pair law is a statement about that relationship: continuous reference plane = keep the spine solid under the pair · symmetry = the two wings mirror · terminate both physical ends = the two source benches and the crown. And the diff-pair difficulty gradient — keep-together → match + terminate → control-Z — is the mastery rings.

Four laws: current flow = directed prerequisite gating (an unpowered node carries no current → unreachable → out-of-order learning impossible by KVL) · ground = the center + the return every path closes to · a phase = one career spoke · a voltage domain = a bounded ring at one potential.

The five operator calls — locked

  1. ElectricalCore is off the per-node critical path. Each wing advances independently, node-by-node; only the rank-up ring-balance needs both legs. The KING never stalls.
  2. Early electrical grade = connectivity / continuity only (does it close the loop, not solved values) until the model hardens on those very rungs.
  3. Control / PLC stays subordinate — the convergence center + a local puzzle; never a co-equal domain (which would revive the rejected 3-phase-as-spine reading).
  4. Every rung is a realism-parameterized variant of ONE flagship subcircuit — the concentric "same circuit, higher realism" unit. No bespoke levels.
  5. One netlist, two views — this full-screen web page and the 3D board-menu, with pcbcore authoring the menu board. "The menu is the map."

What the web forces — build order

The load-bearing mechanic is #1; it is pure data over the DAG, no risky new engine code. Everything else layers on.

  1. Grade-gated current-flow ALLOCATOR — a node lights only if a continuous path to a source exists AND its prereqs are graded ≥ bar.
  2. Two-render isomorphism — web page & 3D board-menu are ONE netlist (IC-to-IC travel == pathing the DAG).
  3. Wheatstone-null RING grader — thin logic over GF_Inspector; electrical leg on the lighter provisional bar early.
  4. "Close-the-loop" completion verb — net-closed (DRC 0/0) & energized (IsLive) collapse to one continuity check.
  5. Shared 45° routing head proven identical between pcbcore & the facility router.
  6. Parametric socket layer — drop a distill variant (2-layer↔6-layer) into the same notable.
  7. Interleave-bridge authoring — a first-class content type: re-test an earlier competency in a new context.
  8. Balanced-party co-op on the card-edge cluster bus (SYNC_CLK lockstep; idle player = neutral-current inefficiency).
  9. Career-crown tap + Δ-vs-Y late keystone — last; the reward tier.
  10. RULES OUT — fungible point-buy · travel-filler · XP-only unlocks · respec of knowledge · lattice cross-links for looks.
